    Reflection

    My design choices, challenges and lessons learned.

    Design choices

    I chose pink and white because pink is my favourite colour and shows my personality. White keeps the pages clean and easy to read. I used rounded cards and simple layouts so the website feels friendly and is easy to understand. I also added a text-only button so everyone can read my pages easily.

    Challenges

    1. Making every page look the same (menu and footer).
    2. Finding the right coordinates for the image map.
    3. Making the website fit on a phone screen.
    4. Uploading the website to GitHub for the first time.

    Lessons learned

    • Plan the pages and folders before coding.
    • Test every link and image.
    • Small mistakes are normal, so keep trying.
    Be yourself no matter who you are, even in code.
